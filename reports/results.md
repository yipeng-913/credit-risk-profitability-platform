# Results · synthetic demonstration only

1,800 independent synthetic applicants; train 1,080 / calibration 360 / hold-out 360. No real Home Credit model performance is claimed.

| Final calibrated model | ROC-AUC | Average precision | Brier | Hold-out event rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Logistic Regression | 0.6284 | 0.2051 | 0.1008 | 0.1167 |

Training-only 3-fold CV compares Logistic Regression and LightGBM; selection uses average precision, never hold-out metrics. See [training manifest](demo/training.json). Calibration uses a separate 20% partition. Threshold 0.20 was fixed as a demonstration: TN 290 / FP 28 / FN 35 / TP 7. No fairness, cost-optimality or deployment validity is established.

Recommendations: validate affordability/external-score signals on timestamped bank data; review calibration and error costs by borrower segment before setting policy; stress funding and risk assumptions jointly and replace hypothetical finance inputs with observed cash flows. These are recommendations for validation and analysis, not borrower-level decisions.
